About this dashboard

HOODIEGHOST GROWS is a local grow-room organizer. It does not connect to sensors, control equipment, or automatically verify environmental conditions. Alerts compare your entered readings with the ranges you configure.

This version saves to browser storage on the current device. Cloud sync and the AI assistant are not connected in this rebuild.
